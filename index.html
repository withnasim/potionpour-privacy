Potion Pour Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 October 2026

Potion Pour ("the app") is a single-player color sorting puzzle game by Najura, a brand of Najuvia LLC. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and the choices you have.

Information collected

The app has no accounts and never asks for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location. It uses two Google services that collect some information automatically:

  • Google AdMob (advertising): shows ads in the game. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, general location derived from the IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase (GA4): helps us understand how the game is played so we can improve it. It collects app-usage events (for example levels started and finished, hints used and settings changed), device and app information, an app-instance ID and, where allowed, the advertising ID.

How information is used

To show ads (personalized or non-personalized, depending on your choices), to measure ad performance, to prevent fraud, to understand how the game is used, and to fix problems and improve the game. We do not sell personal information.

Sharing

Information is processed by Google as described above. Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Your choices

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland or another region where consent is required, the app asks for your consent the first time you play. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy choices.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android device settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads).
  • Rewarded videos are optional: you only see one if you choose to watch it (for example to get extra hints, one more spare flask, a doubled mission reward, or a cute scene for a week).
  • Ads in the app are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

Data stored on your device

Your level progress, stars, hints, missions, unlocked scenes, daily streak, settings and current puzzle are saved on your device. If Android backup is on, Android may include this game data in your device backup, which is handled by Google under your Google account settings.

Data retention and deletion

Google Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months. You can delete game data on your device with Settings › Reset progress, by clearing the app's storage, or by uninstalling the app. To ask about analytics data linked to your app-instance ID, contact us at the address below.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same address and in the app, with a new effective date.

Contact
