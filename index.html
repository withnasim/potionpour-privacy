Potion Pour Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 October 2026

Potion Pour ("the app") is a single-player color sorting puzzle game. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or device information. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no crash-reporting services, and it does not request internet access or any device permissions.

Data stored on your device

Your level progress, stars, daily streak, settings and current puzzle are saved only on your device so you can continue playing. If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include this game data in your device backup, which is handled by Google under your Google account settings. We never receive it.

Deleting your data

You can delete all game data at any time with Settings › Reset progress, or by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Children

The app is suitable for players of all ages. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13, and it contains no ads, external links or in-app purchases.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address and in the app, with a new effective date.
